# Original high-temperature teaching assets

Generated by tools/build_high_temp_assets.py. Source is editable high_temp_assets.blend; meshes are original, with no downloaded CAD or external texture dependencies. Only new V19 assets are written. Blender metre geometry converts once at the exported root, 0.1 world units per metre.

## Parabolic trough

EuroTrough-inspired single 12 m north–south teaching segment, 5.77 m aperture, 69.24 m² aperture per module. Cross-section y=x²/(4×1.71); receiver centre is exactly 1.71 m above the vertex. Nominal receiver diameter is 70 mm (teaching geometry). Optical assembly turns about a horizontal north–south axis at 3.5 m above grade. The 3.25 m full-circle sweep radius covers rim, receiver and ribs; conservative module envelope is 6.5 × 13 m, height 6.75 m. Module gaps 2 m and perimeter access 2 m are teaching allowances, not certified installation/fire clearances. Thermal conditions are fixed-grade Therminol 66 at 300/200°C; no tower, salt, steam or electricity generation is implied.

Required module count is ceil(requested aperture /69.24). A fractionally utilized last segment remains full physical size. Geometry never compresses to fit the station. Tracker consumes altitude/azimuth, azimuth clockwise from north; below the horizon it stows upward. Ground piping and endpoints remain fixed.

## Direct two-tank oil inventory

Each vessel has H=D. Per MWhth: mass=3,600,000/238 kg, full hot volume=mass/809 m³ (18.697...), geometrical tank volume=hot volume/0.8 (23.372...). BOTH hot and cold tanks independently accommodate full hot-volume inventory plus 20% freeboard. This is one transferable inventory, not two inventories credited as capacity. Cold tank sized using hot expansion is conservative. Default asset is 0.2 MWhth; custom capacities use the identical metric cylinder formula procedurally, maintaining 2 m separation and 2 m perimeter access. SOC is a separate annotation; it does not change tank temperature or create a mixing model.

## Oil/water exchanger

Original 3 × 2 × 1.8 m shell-and-tube skid per 0.1 MWth; modules increase with duty rather than shrink. Distinct oil hot_in (300/200°C), water warm_out (90/60°C), electricity ac_in for circulation. Logical heat ports represent paired supply/return circuits; fluids remain isolated. Physical dimensions/rating are teaching assumptions, not a manufacturer selection or pressure-vessel design.
